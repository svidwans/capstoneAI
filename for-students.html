Course Structure — 4 Phases
Phase 1
Weeks 1–3

Discovery & Tool Training

Client discovery and no-code AI tool labs run concurrently. Students scope the client problem while learning the tools they'll need to solve it.

Phase 2
Weeks 4–5

Solution Design

Teams propose, scope, and get sign-off on their approach. Scope negotiation with the client is a graded milestone — not background work.

Phase 3
Weeks 6–9

Build & Deploy

The core implementation phase. Weekly Loom stand-ups, decision logs, and Human Judgment Moment documentation throughout.

Phase 4
Week 10

Handoff & Credential

Client delivery, expert panel review, credential issuance, and LinkedIn profile finalization. The portfolio is published within 48 hours of completion.

Grading Breakdown
30%

Client Deliverables

Scored against the original scope. Metric movement vs. baseline — not client satisfaction.

20%

Project Milestones

Discovery report, scoping sign-off, mid-point check-in, and final presentation.

15%

Professionalism

Graded across all 10 weeks — written communication, meeting conduct, ownership, and managing up. Cannot be made up at the end.

15%

Discovery & Scoping

Quality of client research, problem framing, and scope document. Evaluated before build begins.

10%

Client Satisfaction

Client-completed rubric at handoff, weighted separately from outcome metrics.

10%

Tool Labs

Six no-code AI lab completions, sequenced to arrive just before each is needed on the project.

What is a Human Judgment Moment?

Every Capstone project requires students to document at least three moments where they evaluated AI output, made an independent judgment, and can explain their reasoning. These are logged, reviewed by the expert panel, and included in the verified portfolio — evidence of responsible, human-in-the-loop AI use that no transcript or certificate can provide.
