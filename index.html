The Problem

One solution. Three beneficiaries.

Non-profits need AI capacity they can't afford. Students need real-world experience employers will recognize. And employers need to hire people who've actually worked with AI — not just studied it. These three gaps point at the same solution.

For Non-Profits

Mission-driven organizations face the same operational pressures as any business — outreach, communications, data — without the budget for consultants or a full-time AI hire. Most can't access AI capacity at any price that makes sense.

For Talent

Employers aren't struggling to find people who can run an AI tool. They're struggling to find people who can communicate findings to a client, make a judgment call when the model is wrong, and know when to keep a human in the loop. Those skills can't be taught in a classroom — they only come from real work, with real stakes and a real client on the other end.

For Employers

Hiring managers want candidates who've worked with real clients, made real decisions under uncertainty, and understand when AI helps and when a human needs to stay in the loop. That combination is rare — and nearly impossible to verify in a new graduate without auditable evidence.

What We Do

Three services. Real outcomes.

Each service is built around a defined scope, a standard toolset, and a measurable outcome benchmark. You see exactly what you're getting before you sign up.

Service 01

Outreach & Community Engagement

Reaching new audiences is the most common challenge non-profits report. A two-person team learns your mission, builds an AI-assisted content and outreach system tuned to your voice, and hands off everything so it keeps running after the semester ends. An expert reviews all deliverables before handoff.

How it works
  1. We learn your mission, your audience, and what's worked before
  2. We build and deploy a content and outreach system using standard AI tools
  3. We hand off everything — templates, prompts, workflows — so you can run it yourself

Throughout the engagement, students practice client communication and stakeholder management — not just at delivery, but at every check-in along the way.

Service 02

Data Analysis & Impact Reporting

Most non-profits collect more data than they use. A two-person team audits what you already have, identifies the 2–3 questions it should be answering, and builds a clean dashboard you can use for internal decisions and external reporting. An expert validates the analysis before delivery.

How it works
  1. We identify the 2–3 questions your data should be answering but isn't
  2. We build a clean analysis using standard tools (Google Looker Studio, Sheets, or equivalent)
  3. We document how to read it and update it — so it stays useful after delivery

Translating data findings for a non-technical client is a core part of the project. Students learn to present, defend, and adjust their analysis in real conversations — not just hand off a dashboard.

Service 03

Content & Communications at Scale

Most non-profits don't have the time or budget to produce content consistently. A two-person team extracts your voice, builds a content system around it, and hands off templates, prompts, and workflows so you can produce without starting from scratch every time. An expert reviews for quality before handoff.

How it works
  1. We extract your voice, tone, and what makes your organization distinct
  2. We build a content system — templates, formats, and an AI workflow tailored to your channels
  3. We deliver a complete handoff kit so content production doesn't stop when we do

Students learn to represent an organization's voice responsibly — including knowing when to override AI-generated copy and when the human judgment call matters most.

The Credential

A portfolio an employer can actually verify.

Not a certificate. An auditable record — baseline metrics, outcome data, AI tool logs, Human Judgment Moment documentation, and expert panel scores. Every claim links to evidence of real work with a real client.

What is a Human Judgment Moment?

Every Capstone project requires students to document at least three moments where they evaluated AI output, made an independent judgment, and can explain their reasoning. These are logged, reviewed by the expert panel, and included in the verified portfolio — evidence of responsible, human-in-the-loop AI use that no transcript or certificate can provide.

✓
Capstone Verified

Completed project with Pass rating. Baseline and outcome documented. Client reference included. Expert panel score published. Demonstrates real engagement, sound judgment, and responsible AI use.

★
Capstone Distinguished

Distinction rating from the independent expert panel. Strategic quality scored above threshold. Recommended for roles requiring demonstrated AI judgment, client communication, and leadership under uncertainty.
